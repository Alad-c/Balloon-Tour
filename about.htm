Our History

SkyDream Balloons began in 2008 as a small family business in County Wicklow. What started with a single balloon and a passion for flight has grown into one of Ireland’s most respected hot air balloon companies.

Over the years, we have flown thousands of passengers from Ireland and around the world, always with the same promise: calm, safe, and unforgettable experiences above the Emerald Isle.

Safety, Licensing & Insurance

All SkyDream flights are operated under full commercial hot air balloon licenses. Our pilots are certified by the Irish Aviation Authority and undergo regular training and medical checks.

  • Licensed: Commercial balloon operator license and certified pilots.
  • Insured: Comprehensive passenger and third-party liability insurance.
  • Maintenance: Regular inspections and maintenance according to manufacturer and aviation authority standards.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I free fall from the balloon?

No. Free falling, skydiving, or jumping from the balloon is strictly not allowed on our flights. Our experiences are designed as calm, scenic journeys—not extreme sports. All passengers must remain inside the basket for the entire flight.

Season and weather conditions

We usually fly from March to October. Flights depend heavily on weather: low wind, good visibility, and no heavy rain or storms. If conditions are unsafe, we postpone or reschedule your flight at no extra cost.

Do birds get hurt?

No. Hot air balloons move slowly and quietly compared to other aircraft. Birds usually keep a natural distance and simply fly around or below us. We avoid sensitive wildlife areas and follow environmental guidelines to minimise disturbance.

Environmental considerations

Our burners use propane, and we continuously work to reduce our environmental impact. We:

  • Offset a portion of our fuel use through local tree-planting projects.
  • Limit vehicle convoys and optimise chase routes.
  • Respect farmland, wildlife habitats, and local communities at landing sites.
