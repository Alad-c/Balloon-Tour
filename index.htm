Hot Air Balloon Adventures Across Ireland

Discover Ireland’s most breathtaking tourist sites from the sky: Cliffs of Moher, Ring of Kerry, Wicklow Mountains, and more. Our colourful fleet of licensed and insured balloons is ready to take you on an unforgettable journey.

  • Licensed & Insured: Fully certified pilots and equipment.
  • Fleet Size: 8 modern hot air balloons.
  • Capacity: Baskets for 2–12 passengers.
  • Season: March – October, weather permitting.

Our Brand & Experience

SkyDream Balloons is all about calm, colour, and care. Our brand stands for safe adventure, gentle storytelling from the sky, and deep respect for Ireland’s landscapes and wildlife.

Daily Schedule & Duration

Morning Flights: 06:00 – 09:00

Evening Flights: 17:00 – 20:00 (summer) / 15:00 – 18:00 (spring & autumn)

Typical Flight Duration: 60–90 minutes in the air, 3 hours total experience.

Locations

We operate from launch sites near Dublin, Galway, Killarney, and Waterford, with views of Ireland’s most iconic tourist destinations.

Exact launch site is confirmed 24 hours before your flight, based on weather and wind direction.

Limits & Requirements

  • Age: Minimum 8 years old.
  • Height: Must be at least 120 cm to see over the basket.
  • Weight: Individual passenger limit 120 kg; please inform us if above 100 kg.
  • Mobility: Must be able to stand for 1 hour and climb into the basket.
